Eligibility Criteria

School Requirements

  • Grade Coverage: Must serve students in grades 5-12 (or equivalent)
  • Student Count: Minimum 100 students in eligible grades
  • Technology Infrastructure: Basic computer lab or device access (tablets/laptops)
  • Internet Connectivity: Reliable broadband connection (minimum 10 Mbps)
  • Teacher Participation: At least 5 teachers willing to participate in training

Commitment Requirements

  • Duration: 12-month pilot program commitment
  • Data Sharing: Agreement to share anonymized learning data for research
  • Feedback: Regular feedback sessions with our research team
  • Evaluation: Participation in pre/post assessments and surveys
  • Implementation: Dedicated coordinator for program implementation

Implementation Framework

1

Application & Assessment

Duration: 2-3 weeks

  • Submit application form
  • Technical infrastructure assessment
  • Partnership agreement signing
2

Onboarding & Setup

Duration: 3-4 weeks

  • Platform configuration and customization
  • Teacher training workshops (online & in-person)
  • Student account creation and orientation
3

Pilot Launch

Duration: 1-2 weeks

  • Soft launch with select classrooms
  • Initial feedback collection and adjustments
  • Full program rollout
4

Ongoing Support

Duration: Throughout pilot

  • Monthly check-ins and progress reviews
  • Continuous technical support
  • Data analysis and impact reporting
