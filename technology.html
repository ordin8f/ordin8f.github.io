Our AI Architecture

🧠 Core AI Models

Ordin8 is built on state-of-the-art transformer-based language models specifically fine-tuned for educational interactions. Our models excel at Socratic questioning, adaptive content generation, and real-time personalization based on student learning patterns.

  • Multi-modal understanding (text, mathematical expressions, diagrams)
  • Real-time adaptive response generation
  • Context-aware conversation management
  • Pedagogically-informed content structuring

🌐 Multilingual Capabilities

Our models support seamless multilingual interactions with specialized training on Indian educational content across Hindi, English, and regional languages. We maintain educational context and pedagogical effectiveness across all supported languages.

  • Native Hindi and English support
  • Regional language integration (Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi)
  • Code-switching detection and response
  • Cultural context preservation

⚡ Real-Time Inference

Our infrastructure is designed for sub-second response times with intelligent caching, model optimization, and distributed computing to ensure smooth learning experiences even in low-bandwidth environments.

  • Edge computing deployment for low latency
  • Intelligent model compression and quantization
  • Progressive loading for bandwidth optimization
  • Offline capability for limited connectivity areas

📊 Learning Analytics

Advanced analytics engine that tracks learning patterns, identifies knowledge gaps, and provides actionable insights to students, teachers, and administrators while maintaining strict privacy standards.

  • Real-time learning progress tracking
  • Personalized difficulty adjustment algorithms
  • Teacher dashboard with class-level insights
  • Privacy-preserving data aggregation

Fueling Our Mission: Compute Needs for Our 10,000-Student Pilot

To effectively serve 10,000 students with personalized, real-time AI-powered learning, we require significant computational resources for both model training and inference. Securing compute credits is critical to the success of our pilot program.

🚀 Model Training Requirements

GPU Requirements: 32-64 NVIDIA A100 GPUs (80GB)
Training Duration: 2-3 months continuous training
Estimated Cost: $75,000 - $120,000 USD

Initial model fine-tuning on educational datasets, multilingual training, and curriculum-specific optimization.

⚡ Inference Infrastructure

GPU Requirements: 16-32 NVIDIA A100-equivalent GPUs
Monthly Operation: 24/7 availability for 10,000 students
Monthly Cost: $45,000 - $60,000 USD

Real-time inference serving, load balancing, and auto-scaling to handle peak usage during school hours across Indian time zones.

💾 Data & Storage

Storage Requirements: 50TB+ high-performance SSD
Bandwidth: 10Gbps+ network connectivity
Backup & DR: Multi-region replication

Model weights, training datasets, user interaction logs, and learning analytics data with enterprise-grade backup and disaster recovery.

🔄 Continuous Improvement

Ongoing Training: Weekly model updates
A/B Testing: Multiple model variants
Additional Cost: $15,000 - $25,000 USD/month

Continuous model improvement based on student interaction data, curriculum updates, and performance optimization.

Building in the Open for Collective Impact

Ordin8 is committed to transparency, collaboration, and collective impact. We believe that advancing AI-powered education requires open collaboration and shared knowledge. All core AI models and educational frameworks developed for this project will be released under permissive open-source licenses.

Our Open Source Commitments:

  • Model Release: Core educational AI models under Apache 2.0 license
  • Dataset Sharing: Anonymized educational interaction datasets for research
  • Framework Access: Complete learning analytics and adaptation frameworks
  • API Documentation: Comprehensive developer resources and integration guides
  • Community Building: Active community support and contribution guidelines

Privacy & Security
