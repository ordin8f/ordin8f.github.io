About Ordin8

Building the future of intelligent education through mission-driven innovation

Our Mission

To democratize access to intelligent learning support that helps students master complexity, build deep understanding, and develop the cognitive skills needed to thrive in an increasingly complex world.

We envision a future where every student, regardless of their background or location, has access to personalized AI-powered guidance that adapts to their unique learning needs and helps them achieve their full potential.

Our Vision

To create an intelligent support framework that transcends traditional educational boundaries—one that can help humans master complexity whether it's a 7th-grade algebra problem, advanced scientific concepts, or real-world problem-solving challenges.

Our framework will serve as a foundation for lifelong learning, empowering individuals to tackle increasingly complex challenges with confidence and competence throughout their lives.

Our Impact Framework

We are committed to rigorous impact measurement and transparent reporting of our educational outcomes.

🎯 Pilot Program Target

10,000+

Students in Classes 5-12

Our initial pilot will reach over 10,000 students across multiple schools in India, with implementation beginning in Q2 2024 and full deployment by Q4 2024.

📊 Key Impact Metrics

  • Learning Outcomes: 25% improvement in problem-solving assessments
  • Engagement: 80%+ daily active usage rate
  • Retention: 90%+ student retention through academic year
  • Teacher Satisfaction: 85%+ positive feedback from educators

🔬 Evaluation Methodology

We are committed to rigorous impact evaluation using methodologies including:

  • Randomized Control Trials (RCTs)
  • Pre/post learning assessments
  • Longitudinal student tracking
  • Qualitative teacher and parent feedback
  • Third-party evaluation partnerships

⏱️ Timeline & Milestones

Q2 2024: Pilot launch with 1,000 students
Q3 2024: Scale to 5,000 students, initial impact assessment
Q4 2024: Full deployment to 10,000+ students
